First Aid Tips

Basic first aid knowledge for common emergencies

Heart Attack
  • Call emergency immediately
  • Keep person calm
  • Loosen tight clothing
  • Give aspirin if available
Severe Bleeding
  • Apply direct pressure
  • Elevate the wound
  • Use clean cloth
  • Don't remove embedded objects
Burns
  • Cool with running water
  • Don't apply ice
  • Cover with clean cloth
  • Seek medical help
Choking
  • Encourage coughing
  • Perform back blows
  • Use Heimlich maneuver
  • Call emergency if needed
